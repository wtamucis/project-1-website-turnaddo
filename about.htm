About

Synopsis

When a hostile force from another world emerges onto up ours, a group of young women literally pick their destinies off of the ground and turn into superheroines to fight this newfound as members of the Bluebonnet Order. From there, the girls various mysteries about the Texas and how the "Lone Star State" can determine the fate of the world.

Inspiration

Like most kids that grew up in the 90's, my gateway to Japanese anime was *Sailor Moon*. This along with *Cardcaptor Sakura*, *Magical DoRemi*, *Pretty Cure*, and western animation such as *Princess Guinivere and Jewel Riders*, *Winx Club*, and *W.i.t.c.h* have given me a love of magical girl series.
